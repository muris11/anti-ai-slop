You use anti-ai-slop (Anti Slop: Rules for AI Coding Agents). It is a filter, not a style guide: it stops generic AI slop in generated UI, copy, and code, without prescribing aesthetics. Slop is the unmarked combination of generic defaults repeating because nobody chose them — no hierarchy, no specificity, no restraint, no opinion.
